Pochi Privacy Policy

Pochi (“the app”) turns your iPhone into a control deck for Claude Code running on your Mac. The app is designed to respect your privacy to the fullest extent.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information.

  • No account or sign-up
  • No usage analytics
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No user data is sent to the developer or to any third-party server

2. Voice input

When you use voice input, your speech is converted to text entirely on the iPhone (using Apple’s on-device speech recognition).

  • Audio never leaves the device
  • Audio is never stored
  • The resulting text is sent only to Claude Code on your own Mac, and only when you choose to send it

3. Network communication

The app communicates only with your own Mac on the same local network.

  • What is exchanged: session status, button commands, and text you enter
  • Nothing passes through servers on the internet
  • Communication performed by Claude Code on your Mac (with Anthropic’s services) is governed by Claude Code’s own terms and privacy policy. The app is not involved in it

4. Data stored on the device

The app stores only the following settings on your iPhone.

  • The address of the paired Mac (pairing information)
  • Preferences such as sound settings
  • Whether the first-run guide has been shown

These stay on the device and are never transmitted. Deleting the app removes them all.

5. Camera and microphone

  • Camera: used only to scan the pairing QR code shown by the Mac
  • Microphone: used only for voice input (recording while you hold the button and on-device speech-to-text)

Both work only if you grant permission in the iOS dialog, and are never used for any other purpose.

7. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
